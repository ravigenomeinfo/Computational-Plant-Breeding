# Research Statement — working draft

## Research vision
To contribute to efficient, resilient crop improvement by integrating plant genomics, quantitative genetics, molecular breeding and computational prediction.

## Research themes
1. **Genetic architecture of important traits:** identify genomic regions, alleles and markers associated with disease resistance, stress tolerance and agronomic performance.
2. **Prediction-driven breeding:** develop and evaluate genomic prediction approaches for complex traits and breeding populations.
3. **Environment-aware selection:** model genotype × environment interaction and identify broadly adapted and specifically adapted genotypes.
4. **AI and data integration:** evaluate machine learning approaches that combine genomic, phenotypic and environmental data.
5. **Translational breeding:** connect genomic discoveries with marker assays, validation and practical selection decisions.

## Methods and development priorities
GWAS/QTL; mixed models; genomic relationship matrices; genomic BLUP; Bayesian prediction; cross-validation; multi-environment models; ML benchmarking; reproducible R/Python workflows.
